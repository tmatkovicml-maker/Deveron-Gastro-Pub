Narudžbe sa stola

Narudžba sa stola (QR kod) sadrži samo broj stola, stavke i napomenu. Ne tražimo nikakve osobne podatke. Narudžba se briše nakon 24 sata.

Narudžbe za van

Za narudžbu za van trebamo vaše ime i broj mobitela (da vas možemo nazvati u vezi s narudžbom) te spremamo sadržaj i iznos narudžbe i vrijeme preuzimanja. Pravna osnova je izvršenje vaše narudžbe. Ime i mobitel brišemo nakon 7 dana; sama narudžba (stavke i iznos, bez osobnih podataka) ostaje godinu dana radi poslovne evidencije.

Loyalty kartica – anonimna

Ako označite loyalty karticu, s prvom narudžbom dobivate nasumični kod kartice (npr. DEV-7K3Q-9XW2). Uz taj kod pamtimo samo ukupan iznos preuzetih narudžbi i broj narudžbi – bez imena, mobitela ili e-maila. Kod se čuva u pregledniku na vašem mobitelu; zapišite ga ako želite karticu koristiti na drugom uređaju. Karticu koja se dvije godine ne koristi brišemo automatski, a na vaš zahtjev (uz kod kartice) i ranije. Ne šaljemo vam nikakve poruke ni ponude.

AI asistent

Pitanja koja upišete u AI chat šaljemo pružatelju usluge Anthropic, PBC (izvršitelj obrade) kako bi njegov model Claude sastavio odgovor iz našeg jelovnika. Mi pitanja i odgovore ne spremamo; bilježimo samo broj pitanja radi zaštite od zlouporabe. Anthropic ih kratko čuva prema svojim pravilima i ne koristi ih za treniranje modela. U chat ne upisujte osobne podatke. Odgovori služe kao pomoć – za alergene uvijek provjerite s konobarom.

Gdje se podaci čuvaju

Podaci se čuvaju kod pružatelja usluge Cloudflare, Inc. (izvršitelj obrade), koji ih obrađuje samo po našem nalogu. Podatke ne prodajemo i ne dijelimo s trećima u marketinške svrhe. Na vašem uređaju pamtimo odabrani jezik, sadržaj narudžbe, kod loyalty kartice i, radi lakšeg sljedećeg naručivanja, ime i mobitel koje ste upisali u obrazac za van. Ne koristimo kolačiće za praćenje ni oglašavanje.

Privacy policy

Effective 24 September 2026.

Who processes your data

Deveron Gastro Pub, Ulica Vladimira Gortana 32, 51550 Mali Lošinj, Croatia · phone +385 51 231 234.

Table orders

An order from a table (QR code) contains only the table number, the items and your note. We do not ask for any personal data. It is deleted after 24 hours.

Takeaway orders

For a takeaway order we need your name and mobile number (so we can call you about the order), and we store the order contents, total and pickup time. The legal basis is fulfilling your order. Your name and number are deleted after 7 days; the order itself (items and total, without personal data) is kept for one year for our business records.

Loyalty card – anonymous

If you tick the loyalty card, your first order gives you a random card code (e.g. DEV-7K3Q-9XW2). With that code we keep only the total of your collected orders and the number of orders – no name, phone number or e-mail. The code is stored in the browser on your phone; write it down if you want to use the card on another device. Cards not used for two years are deleted automatically, or earlier on request (with the card code). We do not send you any messages or offers.

AI assistant

Questions you type into the AI chat are sent to Anthropic, PBC (processor) so that its Claude model can write an answer from our menu. We do not store questions or answers; we only count the number of questions to prevent abuse. Anthropic keeps them briefly under its own policies and does not use them to train its models. Please do not enter personal data in the chat. Answers are for guidance only – always check allergens with your waiter.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (processor), which processes it only on our instructions. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. On your device we keep your chosen language, your current order, your loyalty card code and, to make your next order easier, the name and phone number you entered in the takeaway form. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object. For any request call us on +385 51 231 234 or ask at the restaurant. You can also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).
