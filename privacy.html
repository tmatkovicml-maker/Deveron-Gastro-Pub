Privacy policy

Effective 24 September 2026.

Who processes your data

Deveron Gastro Pub, Ulica Vladimira Gortana 32, 51550 Mali Lošinj, Croatia · phone +385 51 231 234.

Table orders

An order from a table (QR code) contains only the table number, the items and your note. We do not ask for any personal data. It is deleted after 24 hours.

Takeaway orders

For a takeaway order we need your name and mobile number (so we can call you about the order); e-mail is optional. We also store the order contents, total and pickup time. The legal basis is fulfilling your order. Takeaway orders are deleted after 30 days.

Loyalty programme and offers – only with your consent

CheckboxWhat we store and why
Join Deveron loyaltyName, mobile, e-mail and the total of your collected takeaway orders, so we can give you a discount automatically when you reach a spending threshold.
Send me offers and discountsMobile and e-mail, so we can occasionally send you an offer or discount by SMS or e-mail.

Both checkboxes are optional and ordering works without them. You can withdraw consent at any time: untick the box on your next order, call us or tell us at the restaurant. Every message we send includes a way to unsubscribe. Guests who do not order for two years are deleted automatically.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (processor), which processes it only on our instructions. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. On your device we keep your chosen language, your current order and, to make your next order easier, the details you entered in the takeaway form. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. For any request call us on +385 51 231 234 or ask at the restaurant. You can also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).
