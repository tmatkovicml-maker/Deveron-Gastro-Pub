Česta pitanja · Deveron Gastro Pub

Frequently asked questions · Mali Lošinj

← deveronpub.com
Koje je radno vrijeme?What are your opening hours?

Otvoreni smo svaki dan od 08:00 do 24:00, cijelu godinu. Kuhinja radi od 08:00 do 22:00. Zatvoreni smo samo 25. prosinca.

We are open every day from 08:00 to midnight, all year round. The kitchen is open from 08:00 to 22:00. We are closed only on 25 December.

Gdje se nalazite?Where are you?

Ulica Vladimira Gortana 32, 51550 Mali Lošinj, blizu luke. Otvori u Google Maps.

Ulica Vladimira Gortana 32, 51550 Mali Lošinj, near the harbour. Open in Google Maps.

Kako mogu rezervirati stol?How do I book a table?

Na deveronpub.com pritisnite Rezerviraj ili nazovite +385 51 231 234. Za više od 12 osoba molimo nazovite nas.

Tap Book on deveronpub.com or call +385 51 231 234. For more than 12 guests, please call us.

Imate li terasu?Do you have a terrace?

Da. Pri rezervaciji možete odabrati terasu ili unutrašnjost restorana.

Yes. When booking you can choose the terrace or a table inside.

Smiju li psi u restoran?Are dogs allowed?

Da, psi su dobrodošli.

Yes, dogs are welcome.

Imate li veganska i vegetarijanska jela?Do you have vegan and vegetarian dishes?

Da, imamo veganska i vegetarijanska jela. Konobar će vam rado preporučiti.

Yes, we have vegan and vegetarian dishes. Your waiter will be happy to recommend them.

Primate li veće grupe, proslave i rođendane?Do you host groups, celebrations and birthdays?

Uvijek. Za veće grupe i rođendane javite nam se na +385 51 231 234 da sve pripremimo.

Always. For larger groups and birthdays, call us on +385 51 231 234 so we can get everything ready.

Imate li dječji meni i dječju stolicu?Do you have a kids' menu and a high chair?

Da, imamo dječji meni i dječju stolicu. Stolicu možete zatražiti i u napomeni rezervacije.

Yes, we have a kids' menu and high chairs. You can also ask for a high chair in the booking note.

Je li restoran pristupačan invalidskim kolicima?Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?

Da, restoran ima dobar pristup za invalidska kolica.

Yes, the restaurant has good wheelchair access.

Gdje mogu parkirati?Where can I park?

Preporučujemo parkirališta Nova Obala, Lidl i Dvorana, izvan centra, oko 5 minuta hoda do restorana.

We recommend the Nova Obala, Lidl and Dvorana car parks, outside the centre, about a 5-minute walk from the restaurant.

Imate li bezglutenska jela?Do you have gluten-free food?

Da: pizzu na posebnoj bezglutenskoj bazi i bezglutenske tagliatelle s umakom po želji. Recite konobaru da trebate bez glutena.

Yes: pizza on a special gluten-free base and gluten-free tagliatelle with a sauce of your choice. Please tell your waiter.

Kako znam koji alergeni su u jelu?How do I know which allergens a dish contains?

Uz svako jelo na jelovniku i u interaktivnom meniju označeni su alergeni (14 alergena prema EU propisima). Ako imate alergiju, uvijek recite konobaru.

Every dish on the menu and in the interactive menu lists its allergens (the 14 EU allergens). If you have an allergy, always tell your waiter.

Služite li doručak?Do you serve breakfast?

Da, doručak služimo od 08:00. Pogledajte jelovnik.

Yes, breakfast is served from 08:00. See the menu.

Koja su vaša jela po kojima ste poznati?What are your signature dishes?

Ravioli s kapicama i kozicama u umaku od rakova i kavijara, ravioli s tartufima, lošinjski rižot od sipe, dalmatinska pašticada s njokima, riblja plata za dvoje, buzara i ražnjić Deveron. Gault&Millau Croatia nas je ocijenio s 12,5/20.

Ravioli with scallops and prawns in crab and caviar sauce, truffle ravioli, Lošinj cuttlefish risotto, Dalmatian pašticada with gnocchi, fish platter for two, buzara and ražnjić Deveron. Gault&Millau Croatia rates us 12.5/20.

Mogu li platiti karticom?Can I pay by card?

Da, račun možete platiti karticom ili gotovinom. Cijene su u eurima s uključenim PDV-om.

Yes, you can pay by card or in cash. Prices are in euros, VAT included.

Mogu li naručiti mobitelom za stolom?Can I order from my phone at the table?

Da. Skenirajte QR kod na stolu, složite narudžbu i pošaljite je konobaru. Istim ekranom možete pozvati konobara ili zatražiti račun.

Yes. Scan the QR code on your table, build your order and send it to the waiter. From the same screen you can call the waiter or ask for the bill.

Je li meni dostupan na mom jeziku?Is the menu available in my language?

Interaktivni meni na deveronpub.com dostupan je na 16 jezika, a AI asistent odgovara na pitanja o jelima.

The interactive menu on deveronpub.com is available in 16 languages, and an AI assistant answers questions about the dishes.

Imate li WiFi?Do you have WiFi?

Da, gosti imaju besplatan WiFi. Naziv mreže i lozinku nađete u izborniku Info na deveronpub.com.

Yes, free WiFi for guests. The network name and password are under Info on deveronpub.com.

Niste našli odgovor? Nazovite nas na +385 51 231 234 ili pitajte AI asistenta.

Didn't find your answer? Call +385 51 231 234 or ask the AI assistant.
